# Gold POS (Android)

Offline-first customer ledger and point-of-sale app for Android.

## Core requirements
- Local-only storage; no cloud account required.
- Separate customer Gold and Cash ledgers.
- Gold weights entered in grams; 1 tola = 12.15 g.
- Gold amount calculated from the transaction's saved rate per tola.
- Cash transactions recorded in PKR.
- Customer statements, PDF receipts, PIN protection, recycle bin, and manual backup/restore.
- English interface with Black + Gold and White + Gold themes.
